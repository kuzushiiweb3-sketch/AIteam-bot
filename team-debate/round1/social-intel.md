# Round 1: Social intel (X, KOLs, Telegram/Discord, narrative)

**In plain English (for Paul):**
- Hype on X is the easiest thing in crypto to fake, and the official X data feed is now priced per post (about half a cent each), which is far too expensive for scanning every coin. So in v1, social data never *triggers* a trade. It only nudges the score of a coin that has already passed the on-chain safety checks.
- The cheapest and most honest KOL signal is **on-chain**: watching the actual wallets of the traders you trust. A real buy costs them real money. A tweet costs nothing. Watching wallets costs close to $0 extra.
- For tweets, v1 uses a cheap third-party reader (about $0.02 per coin checked, prepaid with a hard cap) and only looks at coins that already passed the other filters. Hype only counts when several **independent** sources agree: different people, not one bot farm or one paid campaign.
- Telegram/Discord "call" channels, LunarCrush and Kaito are left out of v1. They are too risky, too slow, or too expensive for what they add. We add them only if the paper-trading logs show they help.

Jargon: **KOL** = "key opinion leader", a crypto influencer or well-known trader. **CA** = a coin's contract address (its unique ID on Solana). **API** = a paid data feed a program can read. **Rate limit** = the maximum number of requests per time window. **ToS** = terms of service. **Webhook/stream** = the provider pushes new data to us the moment it appears, so we don't have to keep asking.

Network note: WebFetch was refused for `docs.x.com`, `docs.socialdata.tools` and `twitterapi.io`, so almost every price below comes from search-result snippets of those pages (VERIFIED-SEARCH). Two twitterapi.io pages were read from a third-party GitHub mirror on `raw.githubusercontent.com`, scraped 2026-02-08, and are tagged as such.

## 2. What "independent confirmation" means

A coin's hype is **confirmed** only when it shows at least **3 independent clusters** within 30 minutes, and at least **1 of them comes from a different channel** (for example, a tracked KOL wallet buy plus 2 separate groups of X posters). All numbers are Paul-tunable starting values. Two mentions are **not** independent if:
1. **Same cluster.** The accounts post near-identical text, or retweet/quote the same post within about 60 seconds of each other more than once. Academic co-tweet and co-retweet methods use windows of roughly 10–60 seconds, and window length matters most [VERIFIED-SEARCH: [CIB guide](https://www.xpoz.ai/blog/guides/detecting-coordinated-inauthentic-behavior-technical-guide/), [AIDEM 2026 paper](https://aidem2026.isti.cnr.it/papers/id492.pdf), [arXiv 2107.02588](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.02588v1)]. Small groups that all follow each other also count as one cluster.
2. **Same money.** On-chain wallets that were funded from the same source count as one cluster. The Sharpei rug spread 60% of supply over 100+ addresses that later consolidated [VERIFIED-SEARCH: [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/288160/solana-meme-coin-sharpei-epic-rug-pull)].
3. **The coin's own team.** The dev, the coin's own X account or community, and replies under its own posts never count.
4. **Paid or shotgun shillers.** Accounts that posted the CAs of 5 or more different coins in the last 24 hours, accounts on our paid-promo list, and posts tagged ad or partner. Undisclosed paid promos are common [UNVERIFIED: search snippets cite a ZachXBT spreadsheet of 200+ paid influencers, but the original source was not pinned].
5. **Below the quality floor.** Accounts younger than 90 days, with more than 2 username changes, or with tiny engagement relative to their follower count. X's "About this account" page shows the creation date and the number of username changes [VERIFIED-SEARCH: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/x-rolls-out-about-this-account-feature-that-displays-a-profiles-country-of-origin-and-more-160617187.html)]. Whether the API exposes the username-change count is [UNVERIFIED].

**Scoring.** Each cluster gets a weight: `quality (0–1) × originality (1.0 for an original post, 0.3 for a copy or retweet) × reach (log of followers, capped) × track record (later, from logs of how that source's past mentions performed)`.
- Social score = `100 × (1 − e^(−Σweights / k))`. Each extra mention adds less than the one before, so 50 bots cannot max the score.
- The social score is **capped at a fixed share of the total score** (I propose 20%). It can never override an on-chain safety veto. **Social alone can only move a coin to "watch", never to "buy".**
- If social data is missing (provider down or budget used up), the score is "unknown", never positive. Paul's rule decides whether "unknown" blocks a buy. The safe default is: no social boost.
- Everything is logged: tweet URL, the author's numeric user ID, a snapshot of the text, the time captured, the cluster assigned, the weight, and the Solscan link for wallet buys. Paul can click through every reason.

## 3. Fake-hype detection (each flag is logged; any red flag sets social to 0 and alerts the risk score)
- **Bot reply rings:** Bursts of replies from new accounts with default avatars, handles like name+digits, and near-duplicate text. Crypto botnets of 15,000+ accounts use typosquatted handles and lightly edited profile pictures [VERIFIED-SEARCH: [Bitcoin Magazine/Duo Labs](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/business/crypto-scam-botnet-consists-over-15000-separate-bots)]. The v1 measures are the share of new accounts and the share of duplicated text.
- **Bought or aged accounts:** Operators buy aged accounts that already have followers and then rename them [VERIFIED-SEARCH, secondary: [DEV post, Mar 2026](https://dev.to/__747bb5a1521/the-ai-quant-bot-mirage-inside-the-polymarket-copytrade-scam-network-flooding-crypto-twitter-1856)]. So account age alone is not enough: combine it with username changes and an engagement-to-follower ratio. Sampling follower lists is a later upgrade because it is costly.
- **Coordinated campaigns:** Many KOLs post the same CA within the same few minutes (the signature of a paid campaign). Base rate: one report on 1,500+ KOL promotions found only 1% hit 10×, and 80% were down 70% after a week [VERIFIED-SEARCH: [Coinwire via CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/44bd2-coinwire-report-on-memecoin-influencers)]. **KOL tweets are a weak signal, and often a negative one.**
- **Impersonated KOLs:** Each KOL is pinned by numeric user ID, never by @handle. Lookalike handles of watchlist accounts are flagged. A blue check is not proof, because it can be bought [UNVERIFIED].
- **Deleted tweets:** We store a snapshot at capture time and re-check the top 5 tweets per coin at +15 and +60 minutes, at a cost of cents. A deleted promo tweet is a strong exit or red flag.
- **Says but doesn't buy:** A KOL tweets a CA while their tracked wallet never bought it, or is selling it. That is a red flag. Arkham-style wallet tracking of influencers already exists [VERIFIED-SEARCH: [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/arkham-tracking-crypto-influencers-meme-coins)].
- **Borrowed narrative:** A coin links a famous tweet that predates the coin and never mentions its CA. That is a *theme*, not an endorsement, and it is scored as narrative only.
- **Prompt injection:** Tweets are untrusted text. Any AI step (for example, Haiku tagging a coin's theme) may only output fixed labels and can never write a score. All scoring is plain deterministic rules.

## 5. Recommendation
Build social as a **capped, confirm-only input**. v1 = on-chain KOL wallets (strongest, cheapest signal, ~$0) + twitterapi.io on-demand search with a prepaid cap + small official-X truth checks + deterministic fake-hype flags + full link logging. Reject Discord self-bots and Telegram user accounts. Postpone LunarCrush, Kaito and KOL streams until 2–4 weeks of paper logs show whether social moves the win rate.

## 6. Open risks
1. **The third-party reader gets cut off by X.** This happened to InfoFi apps overnight. Mitigation: keep a second provider ready, and treat social as "unknown" rather than positive when data is missing.
2. **KOL wallets know they are watched.** They can buy with a public wallet to bait copy-traders and sell in tranches [VERIFIED-SEARCH, vendor: [MadeOnSol](https://madeonsol.com/blog/copy-trade-solana-kol-wallets-bot-tutorial)]. Wallet labels can be wrong, and KOLs keep alt wallets. Mitigation: weight each wallet by how fast it historically sells, and never copy-trade.
3. **Cost blow-up** if far more coins reach the social check than expected. The funnel size is the data specialist's call. The prepaid cap is the hard stop.
4. **X billing surprises** (the stream over-billing report, and the 2M vs 3M cap conflict). Keep official X to low volume.
5. **Legal/ToS exposure grows** once this becomes a paid product. That needs a review before launch.
